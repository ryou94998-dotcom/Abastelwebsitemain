Abastel was founded in 2019 in Delhi with one conviction: that Indian manufacturers could deliver world-class quality at Indian prices — without compromise.

Starting with fiber optic products for FTTX and CATV networks, we built our manufacturing facility from the ground up. The state-of-the-art plant in Delhi now produces high-quality fiber patch cord assemblies and accessories that meet global standards. Our founders — technically fertile minds with hands-on experience — hired for creativity over credentials, and that instinct still shapes every hire we make and every product we ship.
