Where It Began

2019 — A company with something to prove.

Founded
2019
Headquarters
Delhi, India
First Vertical
Telecom & Fiber

Abastel was founded in 2019 in Delhi with one conviction: that Indian businesses could deliver world-class quality at Indian prices — without compromise.

We began as a trader of telecom and fiber optic products for FTTX and CATV networks — learning the market from the inside and earning the trust of customers across India. In 2021 we made the leap from trading toward manufacturing by owning our own moulds, and in 2022 we opened our first injection moulding factory in Delhi. Our founders — technically fertile minds with hands-on experience — hired for creativity over credentials, and that instinct still shapes every hire we make and every product we ship.
